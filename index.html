Upload an image, then ① crop to the area to analyze → ② click the 5mm mark on the ruler → ③ click the 15mm mark. Because those two points are exactly 10mm apart, the scale and the 5–15mm analysis band are set in one step. The lithium plating area ratio (%) is then computed with the same Otsu auto-threshold algorithm as ImageJ and plugged straight into E81C = (958 × area %) / 30656.
